Course Changes for PUBH132

Impact: Medium

The course has officially transitioned from development to pilot status, signaling its readiness for initial student delivery through cleaned-up Week 1 module navigation and refined question bank prompts. For students, assessment expectations are now significantly more focused, as rubric updates cut video submission durations in half and introduce stricter criteria regarding textbook questions, source explanations, and complete sentence formatting. These changes streamline the onboarding experience for learners while establishing clear, enforceable grading standards for the instructional team.
